1. BODY MORPHS RULE -- CORRECTING THE OVERBROAD STATEMENT
==============================================================================
FINDING: Already fully settled by existing doctrine; no new rule needed,
only a correction to how the calibration document stated it.

The live philosophy is explicit on two independent, converging points:

- CLOTHING's own definition already includes shape as an in-scope function:
  "Clothing contains controls whose primary animator-facing purpose is
  manipulating, fitting, opening, closing, hiding, exposing, SHAPING, or
  otherwise working with garments and worn accessories."
- OBJECT-SPECIFIC PURPOSE TAKES PRECEDENCE states the general principle
  directly: "A control that exists to fit, repair, hide, or accommodate a
  particular animator-facing object belongs with that object," and BODY
  SHAPE's own text distinguishes intended appearance change from the
  purpose test, not from a bare word list ("Body Shape represents anatomy
  or appearance change as the intended result").

So the calibration's "explicit shape/size descriptors with no posing verb
go straight to Body Morphs" was incomplete: it never asked WHOSE shape is
changing. The corrected rule:

  A shape/size descriptor (bigger, wider, thicker, shrink, muscular, tone,
  thickness, size) indicates Body Morphs ONLY when its primary referent is
  the character's own body/anatomy (Hips, Breast, Feet, Penis, Waist, Shin,
  Shoulder, etc.). When the SAME kind of descriptor modifies a named worn
  object/garment/accessory as its primary referent (Boots2Thicker, BraShape,
  JacketBelly, GloveMuscles, BeltWidth), the control belongs with that
  object's own destination (typically Clothing), per Clothing's own
  "shaping" language and the Object-Specific-Purpose-Takes-Precedence rule
  -- regardless of the fact that the visible effect sounds like a body-
  shape change.

The two pilot families originally resolved under the old, broader statement
(Shin Thickness -> Body Morphs, Shouldershrink -> Body Morphs) remain
correctly resolved: both name a body region (shin, shoulder) as the primary
referent, with no worn-object noun present. No re-classification needed for
those two; only the standing rule's wording needed the object-ownership
test added before scaling to 13,782 more families where a garment-shape
literal is far more likely to appear.

2. BARE STRUCTURAL HEAD / SKULL / SPINE
==============================================================================
FINDING: Head/skull -- RESOLVED (5 of 5). Spine -- REMAINS a genuine
doctrine question (0 of 5), for a documented, evidence-based reason.

Tested proposition: a clearly structural literal naming Head/Skull/Spine/
Pelvis/Ribcage/Torso should default to Body unless the complete literal
gives stronger evidence for another established destination.

HEAD/SKULL: Confirmed correct by direct inspection of current Master
content. Word-boundary "head" resolves 2,705 times in the current Master,
and the overwhelming majority (Lower/Upper/Mid Face, Eyes, Tongue, Hair)
is explained entirely by "head [specific facial feature]" compounds (e.g.
"head eyelid left upper", "head eyeball left") -- i.e. "head" functions as
a location/context prefix, and the ACTUAL feature word already resolves the
destination correctly under existing Facial Taxonomy doctrine. None of the
5 pilot cases (head back 1, m_head_2, r_head_3, skull_06, UpperHead) name a
specific facial feature -- they are bare structural/positional head/skull
references. The closest existing structural analogue, bare "pelvis," is
66% Body (139/210) with the remaining minority explained by legitimate
Clothing/Tail/Legs object-specific exceptions (not evidence of a different
underlying referent, unlike spine below). This supports treating a bare,
feature-less head/skull reference as ordinary structural anatomy by
default:
  head back 1  -> groupFile/Body
  m_head_2     -> groupFile/Body
  r_head_3     -> groupFile/Body
  skull_06     -> groupFile/Body
  UpperHead    -> groupFile/Body

SPINE: NOT resolved by the same default, and this is not merely caution --
it is confirmed by direct evidence that "spine" is genuinely attested with
DIFFERENT REFERENTS in this Master's actual historical content, not simply
filed inconsistently. The current Master's own "spine" usage (gathered in
an earlier Phase 2 pass) splits Clothing 1084 / Body 436 / Hair 341 /
RigHelpers 115 / RigBody 21 / Legs 10 / Tail 10 / Correctives 5 -- and
direct sampling shows WHY: many "spine"-named Clothing entries are corset/
dress design elements (e.g. "Ab-Fr-Spine-RibboneA-01"), and many "spine"-
named Hair entries are hair-physics-chain nicknames (e.g. "backspine.L",
"bip_DownSpine_0_L", "bip_MiddleSpine_0"), not the character's actual
anatomical spine at all. This is precisely the situation the Master's own
CANONICAL-COLLISION COROLLARY describes: "the same exact literal is
genuinely attested with meanings that require different Master destinations
... do not force a resolution. Leave it for deliberate human review." The
5 pilot spine families (Bip01_Spine2_002, DEF-spine.031, bip_spine_0.002,
SK_spine_3, spine upper_R.002) remain NEEDS_DOCTRINE -- not because the
word is generically uncertain, but because it has a confirmed multi-
referent history in this specific Master.

3. SELF-DECLARED UNUSED CONTROLS
==============================================================================
FINDING: Already fully settled by existing, EXECUTED Phase 1 precedent.
All 4 resolve. No new rule invented.

This is not a new policy question. Phase 1F/1G already decided and Phase 1
already IMPORTED this exact policy: 82 explicitly self-declared "unused"
families (each carrying positive, explicit self-declared non-use evidence
in its own literal, per the philosophy's "Useless requires positive audit
evidence" test) were promoted to groupFile/Useless rather than to their
anatomical destination, explicitly matching the Master's own pre-existing
precedent ("unused trash 01", "unused trash 02" already live in Useless).
This is committed, live Master state today (Useless currently holds 84
controls, including that promoted population).

Applying the SAME settled precedent to the 4 pilot cases:
  unused b_l_ankle2helper  -> groupFile/Useless
  unused dogmeat_eye_r     -> groupFile/Useless
  unused f_c_faceroot      -> groupFile/Useless
  unusedhandr              -> groupFile/Useless
Each carries a clear anatomical referent (ankle, eye, face, hand) AND an
explicit self-declared "unused" marker. Per settled precedent, the
"unused" self-declaration is treated as positive Useless-evidence and
takes priority over routing to the anatomical group. This also resolves
unused dogmeat_eye_r's creature-scope question as moot -- its destination
does not depend on what species the eye belongs to, because it is going to
Useless either way.

EXACT UNRESOLVED DOCTRINE ISSUES REQUIRING A HUMAN DECISION
==============================================================================
1. SPINE PLACEMENT. The word "spine" is confirmed multi-referent in this
   Master's own historical content (real anatomy vs. corset/dress design
   element vs. hair-chain nickname). A bare numbered "spine" literal with
   no further disambiguating word cannot be safely routed without either
   (a) a documented rule for distinguishing the three senses from the bare
   literal alone (may not be possible), or (b) a human decision to default
   bare "spine" to one destination despite the confirmed ambiguity, or (c)
   continuing to leave these unresolved indefinitely.

2. RAW "_MESH"-SUFFIXED PROP IDENTIFIERS. Whether a literal that is
   plainly a raw scanned-geometry/submesh name (not obviously a bone or
   flex) should be treated as a legitimate Master control candidate at
   all, separate from where it would go if it were.

3. CREATURE/MULTI-LIMB EDGE CASES WITH NO PRECEDENT. Whether a body-part
   reference with zero existing Master precedent in either direction
   (e.g. "pincer"), or one that implies more limbs than the standard
   two-arm/two-leg structure assumes ("third arm," bare "back legs"),
   should default to Other, stay unresolved, or needs its own decision.

DID ANY STANDING RULE FROM THE CORRECTIVES CALIBRATION NEED REVISION?
==============================================================================
Yes, one: the Body Morphs shape/size rule (Section 1 above) needed the
object-ownership qualifier added -- as originally worded it could have
misdirected object-specific clothing/accessory shape controls into Body
Morphs during the full-scale run. It has been corrected here before any
further families are judged. No other standing rule from the Correctives
calibration required change; the twist/roll, numbering, and helper
findings all held up under this review.
