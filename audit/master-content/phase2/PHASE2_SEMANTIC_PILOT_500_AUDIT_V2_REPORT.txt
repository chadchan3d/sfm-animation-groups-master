2. PLAUSIBLE ABBREVIATION != SEMANTIC EVIDENCE -- 4 reverts
==============================================================================
REVERTED (all 4 restored exactly to their PHASE2_SEMANTIC_PILOT_500_
RECONCILED.tsv values -- OPAQUE/NOT_APPLICABLE):
  Ex_2 | OPT_acs_year_l8 | OPT_acs_year_r8 | whL_2

Rule applied: an abbreviation supports semantic interpretation only when
grounded by (a) other explicit content in the same literal, (b) a
recognized rig/DCC naming convention, (c) sibling spellings establishing
the expansion, or (d) strong, specific corpus evidence. A merely plausible
expansion is not enough. None of these four had grounding of any kind:
"Ex" has no anatomical word attached (contrast RForeArmEX, where
"ForeArm" supplies the referent); "acs" has no sibling literal in this
corpus that spells out "accessory"; "whl" has no sibling literal spelling
out "wheel" attached to the SAME naming family (wheelback_base/
fake_wheel_fl are unrelated separate literals, not siblings of whl_2).

Audited analogously against every other abbreviation-based V1 correction:
"key.s/key.x/key 17/key.9" were NOT reverted, because "key" is a fully
spelled-out word (not an abbreviation at all), and its family membership
is grounded by genuine sibling spellings already present in this exact
pilot (Key.Black_3, Key.White_25, sharing the identical "Key.<label>"
naming pattern) -- this satisfies grounding criterion (c) directly, unlike
Ex_2/whl_2/acs, which had no sibling grounding of any kind. All other V1
OPAQUE->PARTIAL corrections (bone_skin_099, end/beam/shrink, l_horselink,
light 3, lock1, show, squish-zx, valvebiped.topswitch, crush-26,
glass_d3_d, plate 2, stick, bracket) were re-checked and confirmed to rest
on explicit, fully-spelled real words (skin, beam, shrink, horse, light,
lock, show, switch, crush, glass, plate, stick, bracket), not on
abbreviation-guessing, so none of these required reversal.

clear, an unresolved secondary qualifier does not block a taxonomy
destination unless that qualifier creates a credible COMPETING owner or
category. Taxonomy asks "what semantic thing does this control belong
to," not "can every token be translated."

were re-audited individually against this test (full population, not a

6. HOLSTER POLICY SETTLED -- Clothing by default
==============================================================================
SETTLED (all 4 rows, CLEAR/CLEAR/groupFile/Other -> CLEAR/CLEAR/groupFile/
Clothing): Gear.Holster, Holster_Base, l_side_holster_0u_0v,
l_rear_holster_0u_8v.

Policy: a holster is treated as WORN EQUIPMENT -> Clothing by default when
the literal describes it as character equipment/body-positioned gear,
unless the complete literal positively establishes that the holster is a
component of a weapon/prop assembly rather than worn character equipment.
None of the four literals positively establishes weapon/prop-assembly
ownership, so the worn-equipment default applies to all four. This
resolves what was reported as a Rule 16 internal contradiction in the
first audit (holsters listed under Other vs. worn-equipment-to-Clothing).
See PHASE2_SEMANTIC_RULEBOOK_PROPOSED_AMENDMENTS_V2.txt for the
corresponding Rule 16 wording proposal (not yet applied to the live
rulebook).

7. LUBE/WETNESS POLICY SETTLED -- Other, not Body Morphs
==============================================================================
SETTLED: bip_lube.001 | CLEAR/CLEAR/groupFile/Body Morphs -> CLEAR/CLEAR/
groupFile/Other.

Reason: lube/wetness is an effect/material/fluid referent, not structural
anatomy and not anatomical shape/deformation morphology -- it does not
meet Body Morphs' purpose test (intended anatomy/appearance change of the
body's own geometry). This is a narrow, single-literal decision, not a
general rule that "every control containing 'wet' is Other" -- a literal
that clearly describes an anatomical geometry/state change alongside a
wetness word would still be classified by that full anatomical meaning.
No other "wet"/"lube"/"fluid" literal exists elsewhere in this 500-family
pilot to test the boundary further.

8. CROW ANATOMY CONSISTENCY RECHECKED -- Crow_3.Clavical_R downgraded
==============================================================================
DOWNGRADED: Crow_3.Clavical_R | CLEAR/CLEAR/groupFile/Wings ->
CLEAR/NEEDS_CONTEXT/[NONE].

The first audit kept this as Wings on the reasoning that a crow's only
forelimbs are its wings, so a clavicle (shoulder-girdle bone) must be
wing-associated "by anatomical necessity." On reconsideration, this is
exactly the kind of "infer Wings because the animal is a bird" reasoning
the review correctly flags as insufficient -- the complete literal
("Crow_3.Clavical_R") states the species and the bone but does NOT itself
say "wing," "flight," or name any wing-specific segment. A clavicle is a
shoulder-girdle bone that could plausibly be treated as generic torso/
shoulder structure without further evidence establishing that THIS
Master's skeletal convention places it under Wings specifically.
Downgraded to NEEDS_CONTEXT, consistent with the treatment already given
to Crow_3.Phalanges3_L in the first audit.

Re-checked under the same test: Crow_3.Femur_R (-> Legs) requires no
change -- femur is unambiguously a hindlimb/leg bone in every vertebrate,
with no wing-vs-other competing reading at all (unlike clavicle and
phalanges, which both have real competing readings). Canine_Dog_LBrow
(-> Upper Face), Canine_LBackLegToe (-> Toes), HeadFurTop.R (-> Hair),
bip_horn_03/Horn2 (-> Other), l_fin_back_b/TentacleTopLeft2/
Bip_Tentacle.011 (-> Other) all rely on an EXPLICIT anatomical/object word
in the literal itself (brow, toe, fur, horn, fin, tentacle) rather than an
inference from species, so none of these required reconsideration.
