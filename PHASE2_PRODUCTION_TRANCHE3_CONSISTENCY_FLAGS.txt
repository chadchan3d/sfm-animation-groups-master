METHOD
----------------------------------------------------------------------
For every candidate finding, the four mandatory questions were worked
through explicitly (per the Tranche 2 audit-method correction):
  1. Does an accepted calibration row address the same semantic pattern?
  2. Does a corrected Tranche-1 or Tranche-2 production precedent
     address it?
  3. Does the current classification contradict that precedent?
  4. Verdict: A. classification error / B. metadata/reason error /
     C. legitimate semantic distinction / D. unresolved/new-policy case.
No finding was labeled "awareness only" without this analysis.

  *** SUPERSEDED ANALYSIS -- CORRECTED BELOW (see

      ThickerDonger -- including Donger04 itself) to groupFile/Body
      Morphs. The lone exception, mp_Donger -> Other, carries a
      different "mp_" model-prefix convention and does not weaken this.
    - Silkworm's historical reference Master places "donger root",
      "donger 4", and "Donger04" as direct structural members (real

      Cock.Shaft0-10, and bip_penis4-10.

  -- not merely an under-confident classification, but a misapplication
  of Rule 24 to a token that fails its own precondition (ungroundedness).

     decisively: OPAQUE/NOT_APPLICABLE asserts NO reliable core

  4. Verdict: A -- confirmed classification error (not merely the
     originally-suspected metadata/reason-only issue, and not the
