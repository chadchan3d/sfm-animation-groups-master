NOTE ON A SCRIPT BUG CAUGHT DURING THIS PASS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first fresh-recompute attempt used str.split('\n') on the raw scanner
bytes. The scanner file uses CRLF line endings; split('\n') alone leaves a
trailing '\r' on every literal, which silently corrupted every fold key and
caused 100% of the corpus to misclassify as M3 (0 matches against the live
Master). Caught via a sanity check, fixed by using str.splitlines() (which
correctly strips '\r\n'/'\r'/'\n' without touching interior content), and
re-verified: known Phase-1-imported literals now correctly resolve as
already-present. Reported per this project's error-disclosure convention;
no historical output was affected -- this bug was local to this session's
first attempt.

==============================================================================
WHAT KIND OF RESIDUAL CORPUS DO WE ACTUALLY HAVE?
==============================================================================
1. It is overwhelmingly a long tail, not a small set of big buckets.
   99.0% of residual families (14,139 of 14,282) are singletons -- Phase 1
   already absorbed essentially all the large multi-spelling families this
   corpus had to offer. Whatever Phase 2 becomes, it will be won or lost on
   many small, independently-evidenced decisions, not a few sweeping ones.

2. The corpus has no per-model provenance at all, confirmed freshly rather
   than assumed. Any "model concentration" or "cross-model recurrence"
   statement in this report (Sections 6-7, 9, 10) rests on a leading-token
   text heuristic, not verified data, and must be labeled as such wherever
   it is used going forward.

3. A meaningful slice is opaque-biped/rig-convention vocabulary (bip*,
   ValveBiped.*, Bip01 *) rather than free-form scanner noise -- these
   three systems alone account for roughly 850 families and, based on the
   Phase-1 precedent-matching method, look like the single highest-value,
   lowest-risk place to start a future pass: same shape as the already-
   approved Wing/Finger conventions, just larger.

4. A second meaningful slice is genuinely non-human creature/character
   skeletons (Canine, Crow, taurenmale) and isolated per-character rig
   exports (the 7 "<code>-Rig-<part>" systems). These are real, coherent,
   but structurally foreign to the current human-biped-oriented taxonomy --
   they raise a taxonomy-scope question (does Master want a creature-
   anatomy home?) rather than a simple classification question.

5. A third meaningful slice is generic technical/DCC noise (Box##, Egg.Wall,
   Null.###, DEF-/dyng/Phy/OPT_ markers, "unused" self-declared items) that
   mirrors the Phase-1 B4 pattern almost exactly and is likely to resolve
   the same way (technical, no clean semantic destination) rather than
   needing new investigation.

6. The clothing/accessory vocabulary (belt/bow/cape/cloth/jiggle/root on
   worn objects) is real and probably tractable, but it is diffuse and
   attached to many different objects rather than one clean token --
   exactly the pattern that required careful Master-precedent verification
   (not bulk substring assignment) in Phase 1, and will again here.

7. Roughly 4-5% of the residual literals are short, vowel-less, or bare-
   direction-word shaped with no interpretable content on their own
   (near-numbered codes, "><", "v_nn", single letters+digits). These look
   like the natural floor of this corpus -- population that may never
   safely classify, matching the "incomplete truth over convenient false
   certainty" principle already in the Master's own philosophy.

8. A non-trivial fraction (about 1 in 6 literals) already carries an
   explicit technical-marker token (bone, unused, jiggle, root, helper,
   twist, rig, opt, phy, def, offset, dyng, joint, ctrl, dummy, control,
   handle, null, loc, pivot, attach, target, fk) -- this vocabulary alone,
   census-only for now, is a natural first place to look when a future pass
   asks "what should definitely NOT be bulk-imported."

No import recommendation is made. This is reconnaissance only.
