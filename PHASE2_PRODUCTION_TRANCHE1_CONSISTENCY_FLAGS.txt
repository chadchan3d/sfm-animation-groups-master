FLAG 3 -- HIP-REGION VS. BUTTOCK-REGION SPLIT BETWEEN LEGS AND BODY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Across this tranche and the prior one, bare "hip" (the joint/socket
region) consistently routed to Legs (leg left hip, bip hipfront r,
bip_hip_r_1, hip base right, right_hip_cancel_end), while "buttock"/
"ass"/"butt" (even when prefixed with "leg", e.g. "leg left buttock",
"leg left butt 2") consistently routed to Body. This is a defensible
anatomical distinction (the hip joint is part of the leg's proximal
articulation; the buttock is torso/pelvis soft tissue), and it was
applied consistently in both directions with no exceptions found. Not
treated as an error, but recorded here since a "leg"-prefixed literal
being split across two different top-level groups depending on the
specific body part named is worth a reviewer's awareness.
