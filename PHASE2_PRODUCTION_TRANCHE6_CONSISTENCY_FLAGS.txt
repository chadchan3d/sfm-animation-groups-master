PRECEDENT-CHECK METHODOLOGY (applied to every flag below)
  1. Is the complete literal directly readable as written?
  2. Is a recognizable generic real object/word being discarded merely for genericity?
  3. Does in-corpus or in-Master evidence ground a supposedly cryptic/undecided token?
  4. Is lack of destination evidence being mistaken for lack of semantics (Q1 vs Q2)?
  5. Does an established sibling series or coherent naming family exist for this literal?
  6. Does the live Master (brace-parsed, exact-literal or same-word-family lookup)
     confirm, contradict, or stay silent on the current classification?
  7. Is the same underlying word/concept classified differently elsewhere in this
     tranche (or, if surfaced, an earlier tranche) without a stated distinguishing reason?
  8. Net verdict:
       A = confirmed error, correction warranted
       B = plausible concern, insufficient evidence to correct now
       C = no error, current classification is correct/defensible as documented
       D = pre-existing/out-of-scope, no action

FINDING 5 -- "Plane08" marked OPAQUE, likely should be CLEAR / groupFile/Other
================================================================================
Row: fold_key "plane08", batch P55.
1. Literal is directly readable: "Plane" (a default DCC primitive name, but also
   a plain, recognizable real-world geometric/flat-surface shape) + a number.
2. This is the crux of the flag. Tranche 2 QA established that a generic, numbered
   REAL OBJECT (Box10/Box22) is not opaque merely for being generic -- "genericity
   is not opacity." During this tranche I extended that same precedent to
   "pCylinder8/10/12" (CLEAR / groupFile/Other), reasoning that a cylinder is a
   generic real geometric object just as a box is. "Plane" is the same category of
   default-DCC-primitive-that-also-names-a-real-shape as Box and Cylinder.
3/4. Same concern as Finding 4, applied to a different family: a generic-but-real
   shape name was treated as an opaque DCC artifact in one place (Plane08) while a
   structurally identical case (pCylinder*) was treated as grounded content
   elsewhere in the SAME tranche.
5. Sibling: pCylinder8 (P56), pCylinder10 (P59), pCylinder12 (P60) -- all CLEAR /
   groupFile/Other this same tranche; Box10/Box22 (Tranche 2, calibration
   precedent) also CLEAR / groupFile/Other.
6. N/A.
7. Yes -- "Plane" and "Cylinder" are the same category of object (a named,
   generic-but-real geometric primitive) but were classified oppositely with no
   stated distinguishing reason.
8. VERDICT: A (with caveat) -- likely a confirmed error under the Box/Cylinder
   precedent as I applied it. Flagged as slightly softer than Findings 1-4 because
   "Plane" is arguably a step more abstract/less obviously a "real-world object"
   than "Box" (a container) or "Cylinder" (a tube/can-like solid) -- a flat
   geometric plane is a shape more than a discrete object. Recommend the same
   correction (CLEAR / groupFile/Other) for full internal consistency with this
   tranche's own Cylinder rulings, but flagged as a borderline "genericity is not
   opacity" case rather than a clear-cut misread, and left for explicit review
   alongside Findings 1-4 rather than assumed uncontroversial.
