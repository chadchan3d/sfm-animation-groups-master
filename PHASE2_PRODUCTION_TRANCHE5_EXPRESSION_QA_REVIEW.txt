METHODOLOGY CORRECTION ESTABLISHED FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------------
For "shocked" and "surprise," the original audit's word-frequency
percentages (75% Upper Face, 74% Upper Face) were computed over EVERY
literal containing the substring, which conflates two genuinely
different templates:

  Template 1 -- BARE/whole-face preset: the word alone, or with only a
    side marker or number (Shocked, shocked2, shocked3, Shocked2;
    Surprise, Surprised, Surprised 1, SurprisedLeftRight, surprised).
  Template 2 -- LOCALIZED component: the word compounded with an
    explicit anatomical feature name (Brow_Shocked, BrowsShockedL,
    Eye-Shocked-L, Eyelid-L-Shocked, shocked eye left; Brows-Surprise-L,
    Eye Left Surprise, eyebrow surprised left, Eyes-Surprised-Both).

Checked exhaustively against the live Master:
  "shocked": ALL 5 bare/side-marker-only forms -> Face/Expressions
    (100%). ALL 20 remaining hits explicitly name Brow/Eyebrow/Eye/
    Eyelid -> Face/Upper Face (100% of THAT template).
  "surpris*": bare/whole-word forms (Surprise, Surprised, Surprised 1,
    SurprisedLeftRight, Surprised_1, surprised, surprise, plus Emote-
    Surprised, Iris Left/Right Surprise(2)) -> 100% Face/Expressions.
    Every other hit explicitly names Brow/Eyebrow/Eye/Eyelid/Eyelash ->
    Face/Upper Face (or Face/Eyes for Eyelashes-Surprise-*).

This is not a close call decided by majority -- it is two internally
100%-consistent templates that the original word-frequency count
merged into one misleading statistic. The 75%/74% figures were
technically accurate counts but the wrong evidence for the question
being asked (they describe the LOCALIZED-COMPONENT template, which is
larger by volume, not the BARE template that Shocked L/LSuprise
actually belong to).
