BACKGROUND: WHY QA3 WAS NOT USED AS THE SOURCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
QA3 reclassified Belly-bottomnude's semantic_referent, confidence, and
short_reason on the strength of a claimed "verified same-model" sibling
grid, inferred from the absence of an alphabetical sort-order reset
between two lines in the scanner dump. An independent audit
(PHASE2_BELLY_BOTTOMNUDE_PROVENANCE_VERIFICATION.txt) found that
inference invalid: absence of a reset cannot prove absence of a model
boundary, and re-inspection of the scanner file found NO positive
per-model delimiter of any kind (0 blank lines, no path/header/record
markers, only 14 stray bracketed lines clustered at two unrelated
locations across a ~9,000-model, 29,769-line file). The audit further
found that the live Master's Body-Clothed/Body-Unclothed-* template
(a structurally close analog, including "Body-Unclothed-Bottom")
routes overwhelmingly to Clothing, directly competing with the bare
"_nude" shape-target family QA3 had relied on. Verdict:

    SAME-MODEL PROVENANCE: NOT PROVEN
    QA3 DISPOSITION: C (QA3's Body Morphs classification not supported)

QA4 therefore branches from QA2 (the last state not tainted by the
rejected provenance argument) and applies the audit's own recommended
correction directly.
