every Leg suffix (RBC/RM/RBD/RBA/RBB/LUB/RUC/LBC/LUC/RUB/LBD/LM/LUA) and
every Arm suffix (LF/RI/LB/RE/RG/LC/RB/LH/LG) for a letter-length or

decoding was warranted, but also showing the suffix itself carries no
information either way.

noun exactly parallel in position to Leg/Arm/Back within this same

Belt suffixes LA/B1/LC/LB/LD -- all Clothing). This is not speculative
suffix decoding -- it is direct, mechanically-verified within-family
precedent: in this specific namespace, an undecoded suffix does not

"float" is an abstract technical/function word, not a concrete
owned-object noun, so its NEEDS_CONTEXT/OPAQUE treatment (object that
floats is never named) is a LEGITIMATE DISTINCTION, not touched here.

Leg, Arm, and Back are concrete body-part/region nouns exactly like
Foot and Neck. Their NEEDS_CONTEXT rows are drift, not legitimate
distinctions -- several of their own uncertainty_reason fields already
admit this explicitly (e.g. flm-armb-leg-lbd: "family shows a mixed,
unresolved pattern between CLEAR/Legs and NEEDS_CONTEXT for similar
codes"; flm-armb-arm-lh: "family shows a mixed pattern (lb->Arms,
lf/ri->NEEDS_CONTEXT)").
