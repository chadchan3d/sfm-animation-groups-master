2. THE CORE METHODOLOGICAL ERROR BEING CORRECTED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The original Tranche-5 consistency audit computed word-frequency
percentages across EVERY Master literal containing a target substring
("shocked" 75% Upper Face, "surprise" 74% Upper Face) and treated that
majority as evidence for the destination of a BARE word-plus-side-
marker literal. Re-verification found this conflates two genuinely
different naming templates that the Master keeps cleanly separate:

  Template 1 (bare/whole-face preset): the word alone, or with only a
    side marker or number -- e.g. Shocked, shocked2, shocked3,
    Surprise, Surprised, Surprised 1. Master destination: 100%
    groupFile/Face/Expressions, with zero exceptions found for either
    word.
  Template 2 (localized component): the word compounded with an
    explicit anatomical feature name -- e.g. Brow_Shocked, Eye-
    Shocked-L, Eyebrows-Surprise-L, Eyes-Surprised-Both. Master
    destination: 100% the named feature's own Face subgroup (Upper
    Face, or Face/Eyes for eyelash-specific compounds).

Template 2 is larger by raw literal count (hence the 75%/74% figures),
but Shocked L and LSuprise belong to Template 1, not Template 2. The
majority statistic was real but answered the wrong question.

Separately, the audit treated "zero Master hits" for mocking/hurt/roar
as evidence AGAINST Face/Expressions. This is also wrong: absence of
Master corroboration does not override an already-settled calibration
precedent (HurtR, SurpriseL) for the same KIND of word (a bare named
emotional/expressive state with no feature named), any more than
absence of Master corroboration made "spiral"/"pivot"/"tilt" OPAQUE in
the Tranche 4 correction.
