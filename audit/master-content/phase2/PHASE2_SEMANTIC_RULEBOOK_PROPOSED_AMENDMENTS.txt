PROPOSED AMENDMENT 1 -- RULE 7 (Body Morphs), add an explicit root/plain-
anatomy carve-out
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM FOUND: the pilot treated bare genital/anatomical nouns and
root/structural-segment bones (penis, vagina, scrotum, foreskin, nipple,
buttock -- with only numbering, a side marker, or a "root"/"parent"
designation and no shape/size/state word) as sufficient evidence for Body
Morphs. This is an over-broad reading distinct from the object-ownership
correction already made to rule 7 -- it is about WHETHER a shape/size/
state/deformation word is present at all, not about WHOSE body is
changing.

PROPOSED ADDITION to rule 7 (append after the existing CORRECTION
paragraph):

  FURTHER CORRECTION: a bare anatomical noun -- including explicit
  genital/breast/buttock terms -- with no accompanying shape/size/state/
  deformation/action word (e.g. a plain numbered segment, a "root" or
  "parent" bone designation, a bare positional/directional word like
  "up") is ordinary structural anatomy and routes to its anatomical
  destination (typically Body), NOT Body Morphs. Body Morphs requires an
  actual shape/size/state/deformation/action word to be present in the
  literal (e.g. wide, skinny, thick/thickness, shrink, muscular, tone,
  pull, tighten, grip, open, inflate, bulge, hard/erect, smack/impact-
  deformation, augmentation/"job", detail). Explicit anatomical
  terminology alone (this includes genital terms) is not, by itself,
  shape/size/state evidence.

PROPOSED AMENDMENT 2 -- RULE 5 (Correctives), narrow the collision wording
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM CHECKED FOR, NOT FOUND IN THE CURRENT PILOT (0 "collision" rows
exist), but rule 5's own text lists "collision" as qualifying repair
language without qualification, which invites future over-application.

CURRENT RULE 5 TEXT:
  "A literal that itself names a repair/fix/pose-correction purpose (e.g.
  containing 'fix', 'correct', 'repair', 'collision', 'squash/stretch
  correction' language) supports Correctives..."

PROPOSED REPLACEMENT:
  "A literal that itself names a repair/fix/pose-correction PURPOSE (e.g.
  containing 'fix', 'correct', 'repair', 'JCM', or 'squash/stretch
  correction' language) supports Correctives -- the corrective signal
  must come from the literal's own stated purpose, not from numbering,
  helper naming, or twist/roll alone (see rules 2-4). The bare word
  'collision' is NOT by itself sufficient: collision geometry, collision
  physics, and collision-detection object naming are common non-
  corrective technical terms (hitboxes, physics proxies, trigger
  volumes). Only treat 'collision' as corrective evidence when the
  complete literal also states a repair/compensation PURPOSE for that
  collision (e.g. 'collision-fix', 'collision-compensate'), not merely
  that collision is involved."

PROPOSED AMENDMENT 3 -- RULE 16 (Other), resolve the holster/worn-item
conflict
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM FOUND: rule 16 lists holsters under Other in its main text, but
also states worn equipment generally belongs to Clothing (the same
paragraph that carves out the helmet exception). This is internally
contradictory for any holster whose literal doesn't independently settle
body-worn vs. weapon-assembly ownership. All 4 pilot holster rows
(Gear.Holster, Holster_Base, l_side_holster_0u_0v, l_rear_holster_0u_8v)
were left unresolved as POLICY_QUESTION rather than silently picking a
side.

PROPOSED REPLACEMENT for the holster portion of rule 16:

  "HOLSTERS: a holster's real-world primary function is to be worn on the
  body (belt, thigh rig, chest harness, etc.) to carry a weapon or tool,
  distinguishing it from the weapon/tool it holds. Therefore:
    - a holster with no further owner named in the literal -> defaults to
      groupFile/Clothing (worn-equipment reading), NOT Other
    - a holster whose literal explicitly ties it to a specific weapon/prop
      ASSEMBLY (e.g. named as a sub-part of a specific gun/sword prop
      hierarchy, not a generic body-worn holster) -> groupFile/Other,
      per Object-Specific-Purpose-Takes-Precedence
    - this reverses the pilot's default (which sent bare holsters to
      Other); it does not relitigate any literal that already names its
      owning weapon/prop explicitly."

  This proposed default (Clothing) is offered for approval, not applied.
  If approved, it affects only the 4 rows named above and any future
  family matching the same "bare holster, no further owner" pattern.

PROPOSED AMENDMENT 4 -- RULE 18/19, correct the paw-to-Fingers claim and
sharpen the wing-bone test
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM FOUND: rule 18 currently states "paw routes to the existing
Fingers destination (paw is the limb-end/hand-equivalent structure, not a
generic Other item)" as settled doctrine, based on a historical-majority
argument (79% of "paw" hits in the live Master fall under Fingers). The
audit found this treats majority usage as semantic proof rather than
examining what "paw" actually denotes: a WHOLE paw is the entire limb-end
assembly, not an individual digit/finger -- Fingers is the destination
for individual digit-level controls, and routing a whole-paw literal
there was found to be a category error, independent of how the *word*
"paw" happens to be distributed in the live Master today.

Rule 18 also currently states wing-bone terms (clavicle, phalanges,
humerus, ulna) "route to Wings" without qualification. The audit found
phalanges is genuinely ambiguous for a bird (present in both the wing and
the foot), so the same unqualified statement cannot govern both bone
types.

PROPOSED REPLACEMENT for the relevant bullets of rule 18:

  "- an individual digit/finger/claw-segment control on a creature routes
     to the existing Fingers destination, matching the human-equivalent
     structure; a WHOLE paw/limb-end assembly (not a specific digit) does
     NOT belong in Fingers merely because the bare word 'paw' happens to
     be majority-Fingers historically in the live Master -- route it to
     the plain limb destination (e.g. groupFile/Arms) instead, with an
     explicit side marker determining the Left/Right reading only where
     the Master independently verifies a side-specific destination for
     that class of content (see rule 10 amendment below)
   - a named leg-end 'toe' on a creature routes to the existing Toes
     destination the same way a human toe would
   - horn and tentacle route to Other, matching existing precedent
   - clavicle, humerus, and ulna do not have a corresponding non-wing
     forelimb bone in a flighted species (a bird's only forelimb IS its
     wing), so on a bird/flighted creature these route to Wings
   - phalanges is NOT unqualified: birds have phalanges in BOTH the wing
     (digit bones) and the foot (toe bones). A bare 'phalanges' literal
     on a bird does not, by itself, establish wing ownership over foot
     ownership -- flag NEEDS_CONTEXT rather than defaulting to Wings,
     unless the complete literal also names a wing-specific qualifier
     (e.g. 'wingtip', an explicit wing-segment name)."

PROPOSED AMENDMENT 5 -- RULE 10, restrict side-subpath entry to
independently-verified categories
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM FOUND: rule 10 as written ("explicit side markers determine
Left/Right subpaths where appropriate") is too permissive if read as
license to route any sided arm/leg control into a LeftArm/RightArm-style
child group merely because that child group exists. This pilot's own
data shows every ordinary sided arm bone was (correctly) routed to the
bare groupFile/Arms destination, never into Arms/LeftArm or
Arms/RightArm -- meaning those child groups have no verified use for
direct (non-Fingers) content anywhere in this pilot, unlike
LeftFingers/RightFingers and LeftToes/RightToes, which DO hold verified
real content (confirmed by direct live-Master precedent query in the
pre-scale doctrine review).

PROPOSED REPLACEMENT for rule 10:

  "EXPLICIT SIDE MARKERS DETERMINE LEFT/RIGHT SUBPATHS -- ONLY WHERE THAT
  SUBPATH IS INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED FOR THE CLASS OF CONTENT INVOLVED.
  Do not infer that every Left/Right arm or leg control must enter a
  LeftArm/RightArm-style child group merely because such a child group
  exists in the Master's structure. Use a side-specific subpath only
  where the live taxonomy's actual, verified content assigns that CLASS
  of control there (confirmed examples: LeftFingers/RightFingers,
  LeftToes/RightToes). An ordinary anatomical control (a plain arm or leg
  bone, even explicitly sided) may legitimately remain directly under the
  bare Arms/Legs destination despite its explicit L/R spelling -- side
  information is preserved in the literal itself and does not require a
  matching structural subpath to exist. When uncertain whether a
  Left/Right child group holds verified content for a given control
  class, default to the bare parent group rather than assuming the child
  group is the intended destination."

PROPOSED AMENDMENT 6 -- NEW RULE, whole-face expressions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT YET SETTLED -- proposed here for approval, applied to two rows in the
audit candidate (HurtR, SurpriseL) as a data-level correction using an
EXISTING Master group (Face/Expressions), but not yet added to the
standing rulebook text.

PROPOSED NEW RULE (to be numbered upon approval and insertion):

  "WHOLE-FACE EXPRESSIONS. An explicit facial feature named together with
  an expression/action word routes to that feature's normal destination
  (Eyes, Upper Face, Mid Face, Lower Face, etc.) -- e.g. 'eyelid
  surprised' -> Eyes, 'brow surprise' -> Upper Face. A BARE emotion/
  expression state word with NO specific facial feature named (e.g.
  'Surprise', 'Hurt', with only a side marker and nothing else) routes to
  the existing groupFile/Face/Expressions destination. Do not send a bare
  expression word to NEEDS_CONTEXT merely because the emotion could
  plausibly involve multiple facial features (eyes AND brows, for
  example) -- that is precisely the case Face/Expressions exists for.
  This rule does NOT extend to non-expression facial-state words (e.g.
  skin-tone/color words like 'pale') -- those remain governed by whatever
  rule actually applies to their content (Body Morphs vs. Face, a
  separate and still-open question for terms like 'Pale Face')."

PROPOSED AMENDMENT 7 -- RULE 12 REWRITE, so it cannot contradict RULE 21
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM FOUND: current rule 12 ("GENUINELY OPAQUE CONTROLS REMAIN
UNRESOLVED... leave it OPAQUE/NEEDS_CONTEXT/NEEDS_DOCTRINE") lists these
three as if interchangeable outcomes of the same situation ("insufficient
evidence"). Rule 21 (added in the schema-consistency pass) establishes
that OPAQUE is a semantic_status value strictly tied to NOT_APPLICABLE
taxonomy_status, while NEEDS_CONTEXT/NEEDS_DOCTRINE are taxonomy_status
values that apply when semantic_status is CLEAR or PARTIAL. Rule 12's
current wording invites exactly the confusion the audit found repeatedly
(e.g. a real object being called "OPAQUE" when a taxonomy-only ownership
gap, not a semantic gap, was the actual issue).

CURRENT RULE 12 TEXT:
  "GENUINELY OPAQUE CONTROLS REMAIN UNRESOLVED. If the complete literal
  does not give enough evidence to determine either its semantic referent
  or its taxonomy destination, leave it OPAQUE/NEEDS_CONTEXT/
  NEEDS_DOCTRINE. Do not guess to force a CLEAR result."

PROPOSED REPLACEMENT:
  "ABSTENTION IS CORRECT WHEN EVIDENCE IS INSUFFICIENT -- BUT THE THREE
  ABSTENTION STATES ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE. Do not guess to force a
  CLEAR result, but pick the abstention state that matches WHICH question
  is unanswered:
    - if NO token in the complete literal names any real object,
      material, or concrete function/action (a bare number, a pure
      positional/directional adjective with no object noun, a generic
      DCC/software auto-name artifact, or an ambiguous short alphanumeric
      code) -> semantic_status = OPAQUE, taxonomy_status =
      NOT_APPLICABLE (see rule 21 -- these two are biconditional, never
      set independently)
    - if the semantic REFERENT is understood (CLEAR or at least PARTIAL
      -- something real is named) but its OWNERSHIP or exact DESTINATION
      is not established by the complete literal -> semantic_status =
      CLEAR or PARTIAL as appropriate, taxonomy_status = NEEDS_CONTEXT
    - if the semantic referent and general destination CATEGORY are both
      understood, but resolving it requires a standing policy decision
      this rulebook does not yet make (a genuine cross-family ambiguity,
      not a single literal's missing information) -> taxonomy_status =
      NEEDS_DOCTRINE and the ambiguity should be reported, not silently
      guessed
  A semantic_status=CLEAR row must never carry an uncertainty_reason that
  contradicts the referent it asserts (e.g. confidently naming a specific
  object while simultaneously saying that object's domain cannot be
  established) -- that combination means the row should be PARTIAL, not
  CLEAR. Before finalizing any batch, spot-check CLEAR rows with a
  non-empty uncertainty_reason for exactly this contradiction."
