----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE METHODOLOGICAL DEFECT, CONFIRMED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The prior blocker report inferred "same model" from the absence of an
alphabetical sort-order reset between two lines. That inference is
invalid as stated: a reset positively proves a NEW model's list began
(the order broke), but the absence of a reset only shows the order
happened to continue -- which is exactly what would also happen at a
true model boundary if the last control of model A and the first
control of model B happen to be alphabetically consistent. Absence of
a reset cannot prove absence of a boundary. This defect is accepted in
full and the prior report's same-model claim is retracted below.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RE-INSPECTED SCANNER FORMAT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The file was re-examined for ANY positive structural delimiter --
explicit model path/header, model-start/end marker, repeated filename
line, record identifier, per-control model-path field, meaningful
blank/separator record, or documented section-count marker.

Findings, all verified mechanically against the actual file:
  - Total lines: 29,769
  - Blank lines: 0 (checked directly -- there is no blank-line
    separator convention anywhere in the file)
  - Lines containing a colon (a plausible "field: value" record
    marker): 2, both of which are ordinary control-name literals that
    happen to contain a colon, not headers
  - Lines mentioning "model"/"mdl"/a path separator/"scan": 1, and it
    is the literal prop control name "overpass_swingset_seat_model" --
    not a scanner-format header
  - Bracketed lines (the only candidate header-like syntax found
    anywhere in the file): exactly 14, clustered at two locations only
    (line 6949: "[main_character]"; lines 20669-20681: a run of
    "[----(FLEX)----]", "[----Body----]", "[----Boots----]", ...,
    "[pos_asagi]", "[pos_murasaki]", "[pos_rinko]", "[pos_sakura]").
    For a ~9,000-model scan, 14 bracketed lines cannot be a per-model
    delimiter scheme -- at most one model's own bodygroup/pose-preset
    names happen to be recorded literally as "[label]" strings, which
    is a control-name convention belonging to ONE model, not a
    scanner-format marker applied consistently to the file.

Literal excerpt at the only two bracket clusters that exist (for the
record, showing they are isolated and far from the target lines,
not periodic per-model headers):

CONCLUSION: this scanner file carries NO positive, per-model
structural delimiter of any kind -- no path, no header, no separator,
no record boundary. It is a flat sequence of bare control-name
strings with nothing attached that identifies which scanned model any
given line came from. The earlier "alphabetical reset" observation is
not a scanner-format feature; it was a statistical side-effect of each
model's own list apparently being emitted in sorted order internally,
which -- as the issue correctly states -- can reveal a boundary but
never rule one out.

3. RE-EVALUATED BLOCKER EVIDENCE, CHANNELS KEPT SEPARATE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

CHANNEL A -- same-model authored-family provenance: NOT PROVEN (see
Section 2). All same-model claims are removed from the evidence base.
What remains legitimately observable from the scanner file, stated at
the correct evidentiary weight: the corpus contains at least one
occurrence of a coherent "{Belly|BellyIn}-{bottomnude|nudetop|
topclothed}" naming grammar. This shows the template exists somewhere
in the corpus, exactly like a residual-terms lexical-adjacency
observation -- it does not by itself establish what the template means
or that this specific row's classification is settled.

CHANNEL B -- live-Master naming precedent: re-examined directly
against the actual sfm_defaultanimationgroups.txt (independent of the

templates exist in the Master combining anatomy with a clothing state,
and they were previously conflated. Checked separately, per the

These two templates point in OPPOSITE directions. "Belly-bottomnude"
sits ambiguously between them: its compressed lowercase-suffix style
("-bottomnude") resembles Template 1's style ("-nude" suffix, which is
Body Morphs), but its actual semantic content -- an anatomy word plus a
bare clothing-coverage state with no deformation verb -- is a closer
functional match to Template 2 ("-Clothed"/"-Unclothed", which is
overwhelmingly Clothing, and includes an almost exact structural analog
in "Body-Unclothed-Bottom"). No exact-substring precedent for
"bottomnude", "topclothed", or "nudetop" exists anywhere in the Master
(0 hits each), so neither template can claim direct identity with this
literal -- both are analogies, and they disagree.

Given two live, non-trivial Master templates that plausibly apply to
this literal and that resolve to opposite destinations, Channel B does
NOT cleanly resolve to Body Morphs. It also does not cleanly resolve to
Clothing (Template 1 remains a real, exact-style counter-example). This
is precisely a "raw majority across mixed templates" trap, and no
majority is asserted here in either direction.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. QA3 DISPOSITION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
With Channel A retracted (not proven) and Channel B shown to be
genuinely mixed between two live, structurally comparable Master
templates that disagree (bare "_nude" shape-target family -> Body
Morphs vs. "-Clothed/-Unclothed" coverage-state family -> Clothing,
with "Body-Unclothed-Bottom" a close structural analog on the Clothing
side), the evidence assembled for QA3 no longer safely establishes
Body Morphs at High confidence, or arguably at all.

"Belly-bottomnude" remains semantically understandable at the surface
level (an anatomical root, a region, and a clothing-coverage state),
but the available evidence cannot establish whether the underlying
control is a shape/deformation morph (Body Morphs), a coverage/
visibility-style control analogous to the Master's own Body-Clothed/
Unclothed family (Clothing), or something else. Per the settled
doctrine, uncertainty is preferable to inventing purpose, and this is
exactly the condition NEEDS_CONTEXT exists for.

RECOMMENDATION (not applied to QA3 in this pass):
  semantic_status:       CLEAR (unchanged -- the literal's surface
                          meaning, anatomy + region + clothing-state,
                          is not itself in doubt)
  taxonomy_status:       NEEDS_CONTEXT (was CLEAR)
  proposed_destination:  [blank] (was groupFile/Body Morphs)
  confidence:            Low-Medium (was High in QA3 / Medium in QA2)
  short_reason should state that two structurally comparable Master
    templates disagree (bare "_nude" shape-target family vs.
    "-Clothed/-Unclothed" coverage-state family, the latter including
    the near-identical "Body-Unclothed-Bottom"), and that no verified
    same-model or exact-substring precedent breaks the tie.

This is a recommendation only. QA3_CANDIDATE.tsv was not edited in
