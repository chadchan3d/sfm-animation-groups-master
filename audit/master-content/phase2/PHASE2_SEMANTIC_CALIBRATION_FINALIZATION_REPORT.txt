3. MAJOR QA CORRECTIONS (HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The 500-family calibration pilot went through four correction passes
(QA, QA2, QA3, QA4) after the initial freeze, driven by external
review and targeted re-audits:

  - Stale corrective-language cleanup: removed unsupported "(likely
    deformation-corrective)" parentheticals from 6 numbered-anatomy
    rows (Bip01_R_UpperArm016, Bip01_R_Thigh011, Bip01_R_Forearm009,
    Bip01_L_Forearm007, Bip01_R_Thigh013, Bip01_R_UpperArm013) whose
    classification fields were already correct.

  - TaurenMale_HD / OPT_acs_year unsupported-expansion cleanup:
    neutralized referent/uncertainty_reason text that had drifted into
    asserting "head bone" or "accessory" as fact, contradicting each
    row's own already-settled OPAQUE/NOT_APPLICABLE classification.

  - lipur resolved through grounded naming-series evidence: reclassified
    OPAQUE/NOT_APPLICABLE -> CLEAR/CLEAR/groupFile/Face/Lower Face,
    grounded in a coherent parallel shorthand template (lipdl/lipdr vs.
    lipul/lipur) paralleling the fully-spelled lipLower_*/lipUpper_*
    series in the same corpus, and confirmed against live-Master
    upper-lip destination precedent.

  - Bare "bip collar l" corrected Clothing -> Arms through Master
    precedent: the bare, unqualified biped "collar" naming convention
    was found to route exclusively to Arms (clavicle/shoulder-girdle
    rig bone) in the live Master, while every garment-collar reading in
    the corpus carries an explicit qualifier this literal lacked.

  - Body Morphs reason hygiene: corrected 5 rows (AnusGrip,
    BallsDetail4, BellyIn-topclothed, AnusOpenSuitExposedTorn,
    nipple up) whose short_reason cited bare anatomy or a since-rejected
    automatic "no skeletal joint + direction = morph" inference instead
    of the actual shape/state/deformation evidence, without changing
    any classification field.

  - Belly-bottomnude ultimately resolved to CLEAR + NEEDS_CONTEXT after
    rejecting unprovable same-model provenance: an initial correction
    (QA3) reclassified this row to Body Morphs at High confidence based
    on an apparent "same-model" sibling grid inferred from alphabetical
    sort-order behavior in the scanner dump. An independent
    methodological audit found that inference invalid -- the scanner
    file has no positive per-model delimiter of any kind, so same-model
    membership could not be proven for any pair of candidate siblings --
    and further found that the live Master contains a second,
    structurally comparable template (Body-Clothed/Body-Unclothed-*)
    that routes overwhelmingly to Clothing, directly competing with the
    bare "_nude" shape-target family QA3 had relied on. The final QA4
    correction (built from QA2, not QA3, to avoid carrying forward the
    rejected evidence) sets this row to semantic_status=CLEAR,
    taxonomy_status=NEEDS_CONTEXT, proposed_destination=blank,
    confidence=Medium, with metadata stating plainly that available
    evidence cannot establish control purpose.

Full evidence trails for each item remain in their respective QA/QA2/
QA3/QA4 report and changes-review files, plus the two dedicated
Belly-bottomnude blocker/provenance reports -- all retained as audit
history per Section 2 below.
