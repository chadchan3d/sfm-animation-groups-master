Legend used throughout this report:

  [INFER]   a mechanical inference drawn from FACTs (still deterministic, not a guess)

==============================================================================
1. VERDICT ON THE PROPOSED PLAN
==============================================================================

DOES THE PROPOSED PHASE 2 ARCHITECTURE SURVIVE ADVERSARIAL REVIEW?

    YES, WITH MODIFICATIONS

The five-part structure (A: biped systems first, B: creature/character scope
separated, C: technical markers as investigation not destination, D: harvest
semantic islands incrementally, E: accept an unresolved tail) is directionally
sound and consistent with the Master's own stated philosophy (CLASSIFICATION
AND ACCESSIBILITY, NO TAXONOMY FOR COMPATIBILITY ALONE, the UNKNOWN/USELESS/
IGNORE distinctions). It does NOT survive unmodified: Part A's ordering
("start with bip/ValveBiped/Bip01 because they look high-value") is not
supported by direct inspection of the actual literals, and the plan's own
Section C warns against exactly the mistake Part A's framing risks making.
See Sections 3-4 below.

==============================================================================
2. WHAT THE PLAN GETS RIGHT
==============================================================================

[FACT] Master precedent for narrow, exact-shape-matched conventions is
strong and reproducible where it exists: e.g. cape=99.4% Clothing (358
hits), penis=98.2% Body Morphs (846 hits), bow=98.0% Clothing (100 hits),
hair=97.6% Hair (17,506 hits), ass/butt=94.6% Body Morphs (858 hits),
cloth=94.3% Clothing (3,040 hits), belt=93.3% Clothing (165 hits). This
validates Part D's precedent-then-approve method as the correct general
mechanism -- it is exactly the method that produced Phase 1's clean
results and it still works post-import.

[FACT] Part B is right to separate creature/character-scope questions from
ordinary classification. [FACT] The live Master's group list (35 groups)
contains no non-human anatomical destination; Arms/Legs/Fingers/Toes are
explicitly human-biped-shaped by their own sub-structure (LeftArm/RightArm,
LeftFingers/RightFingers). [FACT] A word-level check for "wolf|dog|canine"
in the current Master returns only 28 hits, nearly all in Clothing (a
"wolf tail" costume accessory reading, not a creature skeleton). There is
no existing precedent to lean on either way for Canine/Crow/taurenmale --
this is genuinely a scope question, not a lookup.

[FACT] Part C's caution about technical markers is empirically justified:
"root" is only 19.5% concentrated in its single largest Master destination
(Hair) out of 594 hits, and "helper" is only 27.7% concentrated (Arms) out
of 469 hits -- both scatter across 6+ destinations with no majority. Both
markers would fail Part D's own precedent test if bulk-classified.

[FACT] Part E's acceptance of an unresolved tail matches the Master's own
philosophy verbatim: "Failure to establish a semantic classification is
not, by itself, justification for hiding a control," and "Controls whose
exact names contain too little globally stable semantic information to
classify safely across unrelated models remain Unknown in the universal
Master." The plan does not fight the Master's own stated design; it
implements it.

3. WHAT THE PLAN MAY BE ASSUMING INCORRECTLY
==============================================================================

[FACT] Direct precedent check on the plan's own headline candidate:
bare "bip" prefix in the current (post-import) Master = 17,176 hits,
top destination Hair at only 3,931 (22.9%), followed by Clothing 3,910
(22.8%), Body Morphs 1,213 (7.1%), Other 1,107, Upper Face 1,084, Lower
Face 1,073, Arms 594, Body 464. There is no majority destination at all.
"valvebiped" prefix = 1,492 hits, top destination Hair at 231 (15.5%),
then Body Morphs 185, Body 182, LeftFingers 149, Arms 147, RightFingers
146, Legs 100, Clothing 75 -- again no majority.

[INFER] "Belongs to the bip/ValveBiped naming system" carries almost no
destination information by itself. The plan's Part A framing ("these
appear potentially high-value... established, repeatable skeletal
conventions") describes the SHAPE of the name, not its semantics, and the
data shows the shape does not predict a destination. The census's own
Section 7 breadth table already contained this signal (bip: 100%
self-concentration under its own leading token, meaning it's one huge
internally-consistent vocabulary, not evidence the vocabulary maps to one
Master destination) but Section 9's region write-up did not carry that
caveat forward strongly enough into the plan's Part A framing -- this is
exactly the failure mode Part A itself warns against ("Do NOT bulk-import
merely because a name belongs to one of these systems"), so the plan is
self-aware of the risk but its sequencing choice (put this FIRST) doesn't
reflect that the risk is unusually high for this specific region compared
to alternatives that already show strong precedent today.

[FACT] Structurally splitting the residual "bip*" population confirms the
census undersold its heterogeneity:
  A) 'Bip01 X.L/.R' clean dot-suffixed (Calf, Clavicle, Foot, Forearm,
     ForearmTwist, Hand, Thigh, UpperArm): 18 families, 18 literals.
  B) 'ValveBiped.Bip01_X' dot-prefixed: 178 families, 178 literals.
  C) 'ValveBiped.X' non-Bip01 (Bip02/03/04 sub-bones, Barrels prop): 104
     families, 105 literals.
  D) bare 'bip_'/'bip ' underscore/space form: 799 families, 801 literals.
  E) bare 'bip01' catch-all: 290 families, 290 literals (overlaps A).
[FACT] Sampling group D (the largest, 799 families) for an obvious
technical/clothing/twist/attach/weapon token found only 188 (24%) flagged
by that narrow keyword list -- but manual inspection of the REMAINING 76%
("non-tech-flagged") sample still surfaces items like 'bip_giroscopeY'
(gyroscope -- mechanical), 'Bip_Tentacle.003' (creature-specific),
'Bip_STONE _L_Finger_Globe_1_02' (character-prefixed + opaque "Globe"
term), 'bip_ClockCollar_R' (accessory-specific), alongside genuinely
anatomical entries like 'bip_r_foot_thumb' and 'bip findex00 l'.
[INFER] Group D is not "anatomical minus a small technical minority" --
it is heterogeneous at a finer grain than any single keyword filter can
separate, and would require the same per-sub-pattern treatment already
used successfully for Wings/Fingers in Phase 1, not one bulk pass.

[FACT] The one shape that DOES have direct, unambiguous Phase-1-established
convention precedent is the finger pattern
(bip01[ _]?finger = 30 hits, 15 RightFingers + 15 LeftFingers, both
100%) -- but that shape was already fully imported in Phase 1 and does
not represent additional recoverable value in the residual.
[FACT] The broader 'Bip01 X.L/.R' dot-suffixed shape (Group A, 18
families: Calf/Clavicle/Foot/Forearm/Hand/Thigh/UpperArm) has ZERO direct
shape-precedent in the current Master (a search for the exact
`bip01 <word>.[lr]` pattern returns 0 hits) -- its case for inclusion
would have to rest on the anatomical word itself (calf=81.6% Legs,
thigh=71.4% Legs, clavicle=90.4% Arms -- all individually strong) rather
than on a matched naming convention, which is a materially weaker
evidence class per the standard this project used throughout Phase 1E-G.

[HYP] This suggests Part A's justification ("may represent established,
repeatable skeletal conventions") is true only for a small, already-mostly-
imported slice of the bip*/ValveBiped* population, and the plan risks
spending the first investigation pass on the LEAST tractable of the
plan's own candidate regions rather than the most.

==============================================================================
5. RISKS CREATED BY LACK OF MODEL PROVENANCE
==============================================================================

[FACT] Re-confirmed fresh in this pass (as in the census): the scanner
corpus has no per-model field. All "breadth" and "leading-token" numbers
used anywhere in the census or this report are a text heuristic over the
literal itself, not verified model identity.

[INFER] Anything in the plan that reasons from "this looks like it comes
from many different models" (Part D's implicit framing of "semantic
islands," and the census's Section 7 "cross-model convention" claims) is
only as strong as the leading-token proxy. Two known failure modes:
  (a) A single real model's controls can use no common prefix at all
      (e.g. plain English-word literals) and would incorrectly read as
      "many models" under this proxy.
  (b) A prefix collision between two UNRELATED models that happen to
      start with the same generic word (e.g. two different "front"-
      prefixed rigs) would incorrectly read as "one recurring system."

[POLICY] Any Phase 2 write-up must keep using explicit "heuristic, not
verified provenance" labeling wherever leading-token breadth is cited (as
the census already does) rather than letting the language drift toward
"this recurs across N models" as if that were measured fact.

[FACT] No part of the proposed Part A/B/C/D/E plan text itself makes a
false provenance claim -- it correctly avoids asserting model counts. The
risk is in DOWNSTREAM interpretation of the census's heuristic sections if
they get cited later without the caveat attached.

6. BETTER SEQUENCING, IF APPLICABLE
==============================================================================

[POLICY] Recommend re-ordering Part A relative to Part D rather than
treating "biped systems first" as the fixed starting point. The evidence
in Section 3-4 above shows several Part-D-style clothing/anatomy token
regions already clear a higher precedent bar today than the bip*/
ValveBiped* systems do as a whole. Recommended order, most defensible
first:

  1. Finish the narrow, already-precedented sub-shapes inside bip*/
     ValveBiped* (Group A's 18 'Bip01 X.L/.R' anatomical bones, using the
     anatomical-word evidence directly since no shape-precedent exists)
     -- small, bounded, high per-item confidence.
  2. Clothing-accessory tokens with re-verified >=90% concentration
     (cape, bow, cloth, belt) -- same method Phase 1 already validated,
     now with stronger evidence than before.
  3. Adult-content/body-morph vocabulary (penis, ass/butt, breast,
     nipple sub-patterns) -- comparably strong precedent, previously
     under-emphasized.
  4. The 7 isolated "<code>-Rig-<part>" per-character systems -- small,
     self-contained, low cross-contamination risk, good for validating
     the method on a bounded set before tackling the messy bip_ Group D.
  5. Group D (bare bip_/bip ) and ValveBiped.Bip01_X (Group B) --
     deliberately LAST among the "biped" material, sub-pattern by
     sub-pattern (twist, helper, weapon-attach, cloth split out first),
     because it is the least homogeneous of the plan's own candidates.
  6. Creature/character-scope decision (Part B) as a standalone POLICY
     question, decoupled from any import batch -- resolving it doesn't
     require touching a single control and should happen independent of
     the ongoing classification cadence.
  7. Root/helper/twist broad markers (Part C) -- investigated last among
     "promising" material because their low concentration numbers (19.5%,
     27.7%, 53.8%) mean most of the yield will be splitting work, not
     quick wins.

8. EVIDENCE STANDARD FOR PHASE 2 INCLUSION
==============================================================================

Ranking of the plan's own listed evidence sources (question 13), most to
least reliable based on what this pass actually observed:

 1. Existing-Master precedent, checked at the SPECIFIC candidate's own
    exact shape/word (not a broad token) -- this is what actually
    predicted correct destinations in Phase 1 and again in this review's
    clothing/adult-morph checks. Highest weight.
 2. Structural naming morphology (side markers, numbering schemes,
    dot/underscore conventions) -- reliable for SPLITTING a population
    into safe sub-groups (as with fingers, toes, wings), not for
    assigning semantics by itself.
 3. Naming-system family membership (e.g. "this is a bip* name") --
    demonstrated in Section 3 to carry almost no destination information
    on its own. Use only to DISCOVER candidates, never as evidence for a
    destination.
 4. Semantic token regions (broad English-word tokens like "root,"
    "helper," "twist") -- useful for discovery and for identifying what
    NOT to bulk-classify, but only becomes real evidence once narrowed to
    a specific concentrated sub-pattern (as with cape/bow/penis above).

[POLICY] Recommended inclusion bar, unchanged in spirit from Phase 1E's
explicit no-percentage-threshold rule but restated for Phase 2: a
candidate is eligible for import only when (a) its OWN exact shape or its
governing anatomical/functional word shows a real, checked Master
precedent with a clear majority destination, AND (b) a representative
sample of its actual member literals has been manually inspected for
contamination, AND (c) any identified minority/exception pattern has been
split out rather than absorbed into the majority. As evidence quality
declines (Part E), the bar rises rather than lowers -- a 30-50%
concentration split six ways (root, helper) is not "probably fine," it is
a signal to decompose the region further or leave it unresolved.
