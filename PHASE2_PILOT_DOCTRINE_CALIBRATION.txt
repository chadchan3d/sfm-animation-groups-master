PHASE 2 PILOT DOCTRINE CALIBRATION
Audit of all 104 pilot families marked taxonomy_status = NEEDS_DOCTRINE
Source: PHASE2_SEMANTIC_PILOT_500.tsv (NOT altered by this document)
==============================================================================

PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Re-read all 104 NEEDS_DOCTRINE families against the Master's actual
Correctives rule (purpose-based: ordinary posing -> anatomical destination;
intended appearance change -> Body Morphs; pose/deformation repair ->
Correctives; object-specific fit -> the object's destination) rather than
against the false shortcuts the pilot appears to have used (numeric suffix,
secondary-bone appearance, twist/roll naming, or a bare DCC prefix).

==============================================================================
KEY TEST -- DID THE PILOT INVENT A FALSE RULE?
==============================================================================
YES, on two separate points. Both are explicitly rejected below.

FALSE RULE 1 (REJECTED): "named anatomical bone + number/secondary-bone
appearance -> maybe Correctives."
7 families (Bip01_L_Forearm007, Bip01_L_Hand007, Bip01_R_Forearm009,
Bip01_R_Thigh011, Bip01_R_Thigh013, Bip01_R_UpperArm013, Bip01_R_UpperArm016)
were held at NEEDS_DOCTRINE for no reason other than "it's a numbered
sub-bone, might be corrective." None of these seven literals contain any
repair/fix/correct/compensate language. Per the Master's own purpose test,
a numbered sub-bone with no stated repair purpose is ordinary anatomical
posing until shown otherwise. This rule is rejected; all 7 resolve to their
plain anatomical destination (see table below).

FALSE RULE 2 (REJECTED): "twist/roll -> maybe Correctives."
14 families (arm left arm twist 1, arm right roll 1, arm right shoulder
twist2, bip upperarmtwist2 r, bip001 r foretwist, bip_bobby_l_calftwist,
bip_bobby_l_foretwist1, bipbrk lthightwist, bipflm rcalftwist1, bone-r-
upperarm-twist1, elbowroll l, leg left knee twist, lowerarm_roll_left_0,
valvebiped.bip01_r_forearmtwist1) were held at NEEDS_DOCTRINE purely because
they are named "twist" or "roll" bones. None contain repair/fix/correct
language. Independent verification against the LIVE Master's own existing
precedent (word-boundary search for "twist" across the current Master,
already gathered in an earlier Phase 2 pass) shows twist bones are already
53.8% concentrated directly in groupFile/Arms (718 of 1,334 hits), with the
rest split Legs/RigHelpers/Fingers/Clothing/Wings -- NOT concentrated in
Correctives at all. A twist/roll bone implements ordinary limb rotation
deformation during normal posing; it is not, by naming alone, evidence of
repairing a mistake. This rule is rejected; all 14 resolve to their plain
anatomical destination.

Two further false-shortcut instances were found beyond the two the audit
specifically asked about, and are corrected under the same principle:

FALSE SHORTCUT 3 (REJECTED): "helper" alone -> Correctives-vs-anatomy
question, without checking whether the literal actually conveys repair
purpose. 3 families (helpthighbackr, rforearmex, ValveBiped.Bip01_R_
Forearm_Helperbone) were held at NEEDS_DOCTRINE on this basis alone. None
contain fix/correct/repair/compensate wording; "helper"/"EX" in ordinary
biped-rig naming overwhelmingly denotes a secondary deformation-assist bone
functioning exactly like a twist bone, not a repair. These resolve to their
plain anatomical destination.

FALSE SHORTCUT 4 (REJECTED): treating an explicit shape/size DESCRIPTOR
word (thickness, shrink) as an open "pose vs. Body Morphs" question. 2
families (Shin Thickness, Shouldershrink) were held at NEEDS_DOCTRINE despite
containing no posing verb at all -- "thickness" and "shrink" are unambiguous
intended-appearance-change words with no plausible posing reading. Per the
philosophy's own test ("intended anatomy or appearance change -> Body
Morphs"), these resolve directly to Body Morphs.

One additional family was resolved using an EXISTING, already-stated part of
the philosophy that the pilot did not apply: bip_origin (the root/origin
transform of the whole skeleton) matches "technical rig machinery with no
clearer semantic destination belongs in the appropriate Rig group" verbatim
-- this is not a Correctives question and not a true gap; it resolves to
groupFile/RigBody.

==============================================================================
ANSWERS
==============================================================================
IS THE SEMANTIC ARCHITECTURE READY TO SCALE AFTER TAXONOMY CALIBRATION?

Yes, conditional on every future batch prompt explicitly carrying the
standing taxonomy instructions below. The pilot's semantic READING was
sound throughout (confirmed in the prior pilot report); the defect found
here is a narrow, identifiable over-abstention pattern in taxonomy
reasoning specifically around Correctives, fully explained by two false
shortcut rules (numbered-bone-appearance, twist/roll-naming) plus two minor
related ones (bare "helper" wording, shape-descriptor hesitation). All four
are mechanically checkable in review (does the literal contain actual
repair/fix/correct/compensate language, yes or no) and are now corrected.
No change to the semantic-reading half of the architecture is needed.

WHAT STANDING TAXONOMY INSTRUCTIONS MUST EVERY FUTURE BATCH RECEIVE?

1. A numbered anatomical bone (e.g. Bip01_R_Thigh013) is ordinary anatomical
   posing by default. Numbering, a secondary-bone appearance, or a DCC
   prefix (bip_, Bip01_, DEF-, Bone_, ValveBiped.) is NEVER by itself
   evidence for Correctives.
2. A twist/roll/secondary-articulation bone (forearmtwist, calf_twist,
   upperarmtwist, elbow roll) is ordinary anatomical posing by default,
   confirmed by the live Master's own precedent (twist bones sit 53.8% in
   Arms already). Twist/roll naming is NEVER by itself evidence for
   Correctives.
3. "Helper" (or similar: EX, aux, sub) naming is not by itself evidence for
   Correctives either. Read the complete literal for actual repair/fix/
   correct/compensate/clipping/"volume fix"/"pose fix" language, or a
   recognized corrective-specific term (e.g. JCM = Joint Corrective Morph).
   Only that positive language moves a family toward Correctives.
4. An explicit shape/size descriptor (thickness, shrink, wider, bigger,
   tone, size, muscular) attached to a named body region, with NO posing
   verb present, is an intended-appearance-change control -> Body Morphs.
   This is not an open pose-vs-morph question merely because the region
   word could theoretically also appear in a posing context.
5. A pure technical rig-anchor concept with no anatomical function at all
   (origin/root transform of the whole skeleton) routes to the appropriate
   existing Rig* group, per the Master's own "technical rig machinery...
   belongs in the appropriate Rig group" rule. This is different from #1 --
   a numbered LIMB bone is anatomical; a skeleton ORIGIN/ROOT is not.
6. Creature/non-human anatomy, weapon/prop/furniture objects, and bare
   head/skull references with no named sub-feature are REAL, currently-
   unresolved Master scope gaps -- not classification errors. Continue
   marking these NEEDS_DOCTRINE (or NOT_APPLICABLE/NEEDS_CONTEXT as
   appropriate) rather than forcing them toward an existing anatomical
   group or toward Correctives.
7. Spine-region bones remain a genuine, Master-specific doctrine question
   (historical usage splits across ~5 groups with no majority) and should
   stay NEEDS_DOCTRINE regardless of numbering or DCC prefix.
8. An explicit "unused" self-declaration on an otherwise-clear anatomical
   control is a genuine, separate accessibility doctrine question (per the
   Master's CLASSIFICATION AND ACCESSIBILITY section) and should stay
   NEEDS_DOCTRINE for THAT reason -- not be conflated with the Correctives
   question, and not be resolved by this calibration.

No family beyond re-auditing the existing 104 was touched. PHASE2_SEMANTIC_
PILOT_500.tsv was not altered. sfm_defaultanimationgroups.txt was not
edited. No family numbered 501 or beyond was classified.
