ESTABLISHED CONVENTION: the Master routes a belly-inflate-family morph
to Clothing ONLY when the literal is explicitly prefixed by a real
garment name (Pants, Speedo, Shirt, Suit, Dress, etc.) -- i.e. the
morph is owned by the garment as a cloth-fit corrective. A clothing
*STATE* qualifier (nude vs. clothed) is NOT the same thing as a garment
*NAME*, and no Master precedent treats a bare state-qualifier alone as a
signal to route to Clothing. "Belly-bottomnude" carries no garment name
at all -- only a body-part root ("Belly") and a state qualifier
("bottomnude") -- so this precedent affirmatively supports Body Morphs,
not Clothing, and gives no support for a structural Body reading either
(the Master's own bare "belly-" family is overwhelmingly Body Morphs,
not Body).
