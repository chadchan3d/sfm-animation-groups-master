B. REWRITTEN V2-1 -- PURPOSE-BASED, NOT KEYWORD-TRIGGERED
==============================================================================
V2-1 (as proposed) listed a positive word list (up, down, forward,
backward, movement, grip, open, close, push, pull, etc.) as sufficient
evidence for Body Morphs. This is rejected as a standing rule: those same
words are ordinary POSING vocabulary for actual skeletal joints (EyeUp,
JawOpen, ArmForward, FingerGrip, TongueForward, ShoulderDown are all
routine articulation controls, not morphs).

CORRECTED DOCTRINE (purpose-based, reflected in
PHASE2_SEMANTIC_RULEBOOK_FINAL_CANDIDATE.txt, not yet merged into the
live rulebook):
  1. Ordinary anatomical structure/bones/joints/articulation/posing ->
     the appropriate anatomical destination.
  2. Body Morphs -> controls whose COMPLETE LITERAL indicates intended
     body-shape, size, soft-tissue form, appearance, or morphological/
     state deformation of the character's own body.
  3. A direction/action word is CONTEXTUAL EVIDENCE ONLY -- never a
     sufficient keyword trigger by itself. Ask: does this body region
     have a genuine skeletal/articulation function this word could be
     describing instead (ordinary posing), or does the region lack any
     such function (making an intended appearance/state change the only
     plausible purpose)?
  4. Object ownership still wins: object-specific shape/fit/deformation
     -> the object's group.

RE-EXAMINED under this purpose test: "nipple up" (kept at Body Morphs) --
a nipple has no skeletal joint or ordinary posing function, so a
directional word describing its form/position can only represent an
intended soft-tissue appearance/state change; this is a purpose-based
conclusion specific to this literal, not a generalized "anatomical noun +
direction word" rule. The short_reason field was reworded accordingly (no
status/destination change).
