G. ORDERING METHOD DISCLOSURE (related-control adjacency, then natural alphabetization)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per this phase's clarification, the doctrine is RELATED-CONTROL ADJACENCY first, then
NATURAL ALPHABETIZATION -- not "natural sort with adjacency as a modifier." The implementation
carried over unchanged from the approved Phase A3 plan already satisfies this, because most of
the listed relationship types are inherent to the natural-sort key itself and required no
additional code:
  - Numbered sequences (e.g. Genitals.000...Genitals.011) and root+numbered-descendant pairs
    (e.g. "Vulva" before "Vulva01") are kept contiguous and correctly ordered by the natural
    sort key's digit-aware comparison alone -- verified present and correctly ordered in the
    final Sexual Bones sequence.
  - Obvious exact-name numeric series (e.g. Breast.00/.01/.02...) are likewise kept
    contiguous by the same numeric-aware key.
  - Strict case-variant spellings of the same series (e.g. two spellings differing only by
    ASCII case) receive identical sort keys under the ASCII-fold-based text comparison, so a
    stable sort places them contiguously by construction.
  - The one relationship type NOT already implied by natural sort is left/right bilateral
    counterparts (e.g. "Left Anus"/"Right Anus"), which do NOT share a common natural-sort
    prefix. This is the one place explicit adjacency logic was applied: an exact L/R stem
    match (prefix l_/r_/L_/R_, suffix _l/_r/_L/_R/.l/.r/.L/.R, or whole-word
    Left/Right/left/right/LEFT/RIGHT) pairs the two literals into a single sort unit keyed by
    the shared stem's natural-sort position.
  - Where a stem had more than one candidate on either side (53 such stems, e.g.
    "Anus.L"/"Anus.l" both reducing to stem "Anus" side L), no automatic pairing was made --
    every literal from an ambiguous stem falls back to standalone natural-alphabetization
    placement, per the standing "do not force relationships where the stem is genuinely
    ambiguous" instruction. Verified lossless: 6,631 in, 6,631 out, 0 duplicates, 0 dropped.
  - No new semantic classifier or taxonomy logic was introduced; the same conservative
    bilateral-stem detector already established in the committed FLEX/BONE ordering project
    was reused unchanged.
Result: 5,596 total sort units (1,035 genuine L/R pairs + 4,561 standalone literals),
producing the exact order recorded in SEXUAL_BONES_MIGRATION_CANDIDATE_PREVIEW.tsv, which is
also the exact order now physically present in Sexual Bones (verified in Part C).
