3. THE DCC-PRIMITIVE BOUNDARY (Plane08 / pCylinder8/10/12)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The raw-tranche audit's Finding 5 argued that pCylinder8/10/12 were all
CLEAR/groupFile/Other, and that Plane08 should therefore match them
under the Box10/Box22 "genericity is not opacity" precedent. Direct
verification found this factually wrong in its own premise: pCylinder8
was (and remains) correctly OPAQUE. Only pCylinder10 and pCylinder12
had drifted to CLEAR/Other.

A full-ledger check of every pCube/pCylinder/pSphere/pPlane/
polySurface row (not substring-sampled, the complete set) found:
  polySurface: 44/44 OPAQUE
  pCube:        7/7  OPAQUE
  pSphere:      1/1  OPAQUE
  Plane/pPlane: 7/7  OPAQUE (including Plane08 itself)
  pCylinder:    5/7  OPAQUE (pCylinder10, pCylinder12 were the only
    2 exceptions anywhere in the entire ledger)

65 of 67 total rows in this family are OPAQUE, spanning Tranches 1
through 6. This is the established, overwhelming precedent, consistent
with the settled distinction (already documented in Tranches 3-5)
between a DCC tool's own auto-generated primitive default name (pCube#/
pCylinder#/pSphere#/pPlane#/polySurface#/Plane#) -- which tells us
nothing about what the finished, reshaped mesh actually represents --
and a deliberately chosen English object name like "Box10" (the
calibration-settled "genericity is not opacity" precedent). The Box10/
Box22 analogy does not transfer to DCC-default identifiers.

RESULT: Plane08 retained OPAQUE (no change -- it was never the
anomaly). pCylinder10 and pCylinder12 corrected back to OPAQUE/
NOT_APPLICABLE/blank to match the established, near-unanimous
precedent, including their own tranche's own sibling pCylinder8.
