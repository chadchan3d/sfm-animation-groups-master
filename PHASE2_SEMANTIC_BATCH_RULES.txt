==============================================================================
PHASE 2 SEMANTIC BATCH RULES -- STANDING INSTRUCTIONS
Supply this file, unchanged, to every future Phase 2 semantic classification
batch. It contains SETTLED rules only. Anything not listed here is still an
open question -- classify it as NEEDS_DOCTRINE / NEEDS_CONTEXT rather than
guessing; do not treat silence on a topic as permission to invent a rule.
Source: PHASE2_PILOT_DOCTRINE_CALIBRATION.txt + PHASE2_PRE_SCALE_DOCTRINE_
REVIEW.txt. This file is descriptive of settled doctrine, not a substitute
for reading the live Master DESIGN PHILOSOPHY header, which remains
authoritative.
==============================================================================

1. READ THE COMPLETE LITERAL.
   Judge the whole control name, not a matched substring or prefix. A
   partial match to a keyword is not evidence by itself.

2. NUMBERING IS NOT CORRECTIVE EVIDENCE.
   A numbered suffix or index on an otherwise-ordinary bone/flex name
   (Bone01, Spine2_002, etc.) does not by itself suggest Correctives. Many
   numbered names are plain repeated/segmented anatomy or rig chains.

3. TWIST/ROLL IS NOT CORRECTIVE EVIDENCE.
   "Twist" and "roll" bones are standard rig/deformation-helper anatomy
   (forearm twist, shoulder roll, etc.), not evidence of a corrective
   purpose by themselves.

4. "HELPER" IS NOT CORRECTIVE EVIDENCE BY ITSELF.
   A bare "helper"/"Helper" token in a name does not by itself indicate
   Correctives, nor does it indicate Useless/IGNORE (per the live
   philosophy's own text: "Helper/support naming alone is never sufficient
   reason for Useless or IGNORE"). Helper naming is neutral; look for an
   actual functional signal instead.

5. EXPLICIT REPAIR/FIX/CORRECTIVE SEMANTICS SUPPORT CORRECTIVES.
   A literal that itself names a repair/fix/pose-correction purpose (e.g.
   containing "fix", "correct", "repair", "collision", "squash/stretch
   correction" language) supports Correctives -- the corrective signal must
   come from the literal's own stated purpose, not from numbering, helper
   naming, or twist/roll alone (see 2-4).

6. ORDINARY STRUCTURAL ANATOMY GOES TO ITS ANATOMICAL DESTINATION.
   A control that simply names and postures a real anatomical
   region/bone goes to that region's established group (Face subgroup,
   Arms, Legs, Body, Hair, Wings, Tail, etc.) under ordinary posing intent.

7. BODY/ANATOMY SHAPE OR SIZE CHANGE GOES TO BODY MORPHS -- BUT ONLY WHEN
   THE CHARACTER'S OWN BODY IS THE PRIMARY REFERENT.
   Shape/size descriptors (bigger, wider, thicker, shrink, tone,
   thickness, muscular, size) indicate Body Morphs when they modify the
   character's own anatomy (Hips, Breast, Feet, Shin, Shoulder, Penis,
   etc.) as the primary referent.
   CORRECTION (this must not be applied more broadly than this): when the
   SAME kind of descriptor modifies a named WORN OBJECT/GARMENT/ACCESSORY
   as the primary referent (Boots2Thicker, BraShape, JacketBelly,
   GloveMuscles, BeltWidth), the control stays with that object's own
   destination (typically Clothing) -- Clothing's own definition explicitly
   includes "shaping... garments and worn accessories," and Object-
   Specific-Purpose-Takes-Precedence governs. Ask "whose shape is
   changing" before routing a shape/size literal to Body Morphs.

8. OBJECT-SPECIFIC SHAPE/FIT/DEFORMATION STAYS WITH THE OBJECT'S
   DESTINATION.
   Applies generally, not only to shape descriptors: a control that
   exists to fit, repair, hide, or accommodate a specific animator-facing
   object (garment, prop, accessory) belongs with that object's own
   group, even when the underlying mechanism resembles a body-shape or
   corrective control.

9. GENERIC DCC/RIG PREFIXES DO NOT DETERMINE DESTINATION.
   Prefixes such as bip_, Bone_, DEF-, ORG-, MCH- and similar generic
   rigging/DCC-tool naming conventions carry no destination signal by
   themselves. Judge the remainder of the literal.

10. EXPLICIT SIDE MARKERS DETERMINE LEFT/RIGHT SUBPATHS.
    Where a group has established Left/Right subpaths, an explicit side
    marker in the literal (L/R, Left/Right, _l/_r, .L/.R, etc.) determines
    which subpath a control belongs in.

11. NO SIDE MARKER -> USE THE SIDE-NEUTRAL DESTINATION IF ONE EXISTS.
    When no side marker is present and the group structure offers a valid
    side-neutral destination (a bare group with no Left/Right split, or a
    groupColor/shared-control tier), use it rather than guessing a side.

12. GENUINELY OPAQUE CONTROLS REMAIN UNRESOLVED.
    If the complete literal does not give enough evidence to determine
    either its semantic referent or its taxonomy destination, leave it
    OPAQUE/NEEDS_CONTEXT/NEEDS_DOCTRINE. Do not guess to force a CLEAR
    result.

13. SETTLED: BARE STRUCTURAL "HEAD"/"SKULL" DEFAULTS TO BODY.
    A bare head/skull reference with no specific facial feature named
    (no eye/brow/cheek/jaw/etc. compound) defaults to groupFile/Body,
    consistent with existing Master practice for bare structural/
    positional anatomy. If the complete literal names a specific facial
    feature (e.g. "head eyelid left"), that feature's own Facial Taxonomy
    destination governs instead -- the word "head" there is a location
    prefix, not the control's true referent.

14. SETTLED: "SPINE" IS RESOLVED BY OWNERSHIP, NOT BY DEFAULT AND NOT BY
    TREATING EVERY SPINE LITERAL AS AMBIGUOUS.
    "Spine" is confirmed multi-referent in this Master's own history (real
    anatomical spine vs. corset/dress design element vs. hair-physics-chain
    nickname), but that history does not make every spine literal a
    doctrine gap -- the complete literal usually establishes ownership:
      - an explicit anatomical/biped spine literal (Bip01_, DEF-, bip_,
        SK_, or similar generic skeleton/rig prefix + "spine," with no
        garment/hair-object noun present) -> groupFile/Body
      - an explicit object-owned spine literal (names a garment/design
        element, e.g. a corset "spine"/ribbone, or a hair-physics-chain
        nickname) -> that object's own semantic destination (Clothing,
        Hair, etc.)
      - a bare/referent-free "Spine"/"Spine01"-style literal where the
        complete literal gives no ownership evidence either way ->
        NEEDS_CONTEXT
    Do not invoke the historical collision to justify flagging a spine
    literal that DOES carry ownership evidence (a rig prefix and no
    object noun, or an explicit garment/hair term) as a doctrine question.

15. SETTLED: SELF-DECLARED "UNUSED" (OR "DUPLICATE") GOES TO USELESS,
    REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE REST OF THE LITERAL IS ANATOMICALLY
    READABLE.
    An explicit self-declared "unused" (or "duplicate") marker in a
    literal is positive Useless-evidence and takes priority over the
    control's otherwise-readable anatomical referent, per already-
    executed Phase 1 precedent. This applies regardless of the anatomical
    region or species named -- e.g. an "unused" eye control on a creature
    still goes to Useless, not to a creature-scoped Eyes destination; the
    species question is moot once "unused" governs.
    This is NOT limited to cases with an identifiable body part: Phase 1
    precedent already includes purely generic self-declared-unused
    controls with no anatomical content at all (e.g. "unused trash 01",
    "unused trash 02," already live in Useless). A self-declared unused/
    duplicate placeholder -> groupFile/Useless (taxonomy_status CLEAR)
    even when semantic_status is only PARTIAL (e.g. an unused generic bone
    carrying unrelated attribution/credit text, or a bone explicitly
    flagged "duplicate...unused"). The self-declaration alone is
    sufficient taxonomy evidence; it does not require full semantic
    legibility of the rest of the literal, and it is NOT a NOT_APPLICABLE
    case (see rule 21).

16. SETTLED: RECOGNIZED PROPS/WEAPONS/EQUIPMENT GO TO OTHER.
    Other already holds, by strong and directly verified existing
    precedent, the vast majority of controls naming recognized real-world
    objects that don't fit a character-specific semantic group: weapons
    and weapon parts, ammunition, holsters, vehicles/furniture mechanisms,
    doors, books, pillows, and similar handheld/mechanical/furniture
    props. This is applying an existing, well-evidenced Master convention,
    not creating new taxonomy -- do not add a dedicated Props category.
    EXCEPTION: a WORN item (helmet, and worn equipment generally) follows
    Clothing's "worn accessories" rule instead of Other -- worn-on-the-
    body items are Clothing; carried/handheld/mechanical/furniture items
    are Other.

17. SETTLED: AN IMPLEMENTATION-STYLE SUFFIX/PREFIX NEVER DETERMINES
    LEGITIMACY OR DESTINATION -- READ THE NAMED OBJECT.
    A suffix or prefix such as _mesh, _bone, _ctrl, _jnt, DEF-, bip_,
    Bone_ (including generator-style prefixes like BLD_GEN_, DEC_GEN_,
    FUR_GEN_, LOOT_GEN_) does NOT by itself determine whether a control is
    legitimate, and does NOT by itself determine its destination. The
    scanner having surfaced the item as an effective control is sufficient
    -- do not exclude or hold it merely because its name resembles a
    DCC/export implementation identifier. Read the complete literal for
    the named object instead:
      - a clearly named prop/environment/object component (tent, wagon,
        bed, pillow, etc.), regardless of a _mesh-style suffix -> classify
        by the named object, normally groupFile/Other where no more
        specific destination applies
      - a clearly named WORN object/accessory (e.g. a backpack), regardless
        of a _mesh-style suffix -> groupFile/Clothing, per rule 16's worn-
        item exception
      - a generic/opaque identifier with no named referent at all (e.g.
        "Mesh01") -> NEEDS_CONTEXT/OPAQUE as appropriate -- the uncertainty
        there comes from having no named referent, not from the suffix.

18. SETTLED: SPECIES-NEUTRAL BODY-PART WORDS NEED NO CREATURE-SPECIFIC
    DOCTRINE.
    Words that already name a real, species-neutral anatomical
    region/bone/covering (brow, fur, horn, wing bones such as clavicle/
    phalanges, femur, fin, tentacle, paw, toe) route to their existing
    established Master category the same way for a creature as for a
    human, using the SAME word-level precedent check as any other
    anatomy word -- no creature-specific taxonomy is needed or permitted.
    In particular (confirmed by direct precedent, not assumption):
      - paw routes to the existing Fingers destination (paw is the
        limb-end/hand-equivalent structure, not a generic Other item)
      - a named leg-end "toe" on a creature routes to the existing Toes
        destination the same way a human toe would
      - horn and tentacle route to Other, matching existing precedent
      - wing-specific skeletal terms (clavicle, phalanges, humerus, ulna)
        route to Wings
    Do not assume a creature-anatomy word defaults to Other or to any
    other destination without checking existing word-level precedent
    first -- precedent varies by word (compare paw vs. horn above).

19. SETTLED: LIMB COUNT AND SPECIES DO NOT CHANGE SEMANTIC DESTINATION.
    The taxonomy is semantic/function-based, not limited to a conventional
    two-arm/two-leg human skeleton. Where the literal clearly names the
    function, limb count is irrelevant to destination:
      - a third/additional arm -> groupFile/Arms (with explicit side
        marker determining the LeftArm/RightArm subpath per rule 10)
      - a front/back/hind leg -> groupFile/Legs
      - a left/right toe (any species) -> the corresponding Toes subpath
      - a finger/digit (any species) -> the corresponding Fingers
        destination when functionally appropriate
      - eye -> Face/Eyes; ear -> Face/Ears; tail -> Tail; wing -> Wings
    Do not create creature-specific taxonomy, and do not treat "this
    literal implies more than two of this limb" as doctrine-worthy by
    itself -- e.g. "Third_Arms_R_1" is an arm control despite implying an
    additional arm; "backlegs" is a Legs control despite potentially
    describing quadruped anatomy.

20. SETTLED: A BODY-PART WORD WITH NO ESTABLISHED REFERENT REMAINS
    NEEDS_CONTEXT -- THE GAP IS OWNERSHIP/CONTEXT, NOT SPECIES OR LIMB
    COUNT.
    Standing principle: SPECIES AND LIMB COUNT DO NOT DETERMINE TAXONOMY;
    SEMANTIC FUNCTION DOES (see rule 19). Separately, a term such as bare
    "Pincer" remains NEEDS_CONTEXT when the complete literal does not
    establish whether it is anatomical, mechanical, weapon-related, or
    something else, and no existing Master precedent exists for the word
    in any direction. Flag NEEDS_CONTEXT for this reason specifically --
    missing ownership/context in the literal -- not because the referent
    might be nonhuman or because it names more than the standard limb
    count (those are already settled by rule 19 and are not, by
    themselves, reasons to withhold classification).

21. SETTLED: SCHEMA INVARIANT -- semantic_status=OPAQUE AND
    taxonomy_status=NOT_APPLICABLE ARE BICONDITIONAL.
    NOT_APPLICABLE means "there is no semantic content for a taxonomy
    question to even apply to" -- it is defined by, and only by, the
    semantic meaning itself being opaque. Therefore:
      - semantic_status=OPAQUE MUST imply taxonomy_status=NOT_APPLICABLE
      - taxonomy_status=NOT_APPLICABLE MUST imply semantic_status=OPAQUE
    There is no legitimate exception where NOT_APPLICABLE coexists with
    CLEAR or PARTIAL semantic_status. If a literal has ANY partial
    semantic content (e.g. a self-declared "unused"/"duplicate" marker,
    even with no anatomical referent), a taxonomy question DOES apply to
    it and taxonomy_status must be CLEAR / NEEDS_CONTEXT / NEEDS_DOCTRINE
    -- never NOT_APPLICABLE. In particular, do not code a PARTIAL-
    semantic self-declared-unused/duplicate placeholder as NOT_APPLICABLE
    merely because it lacks an anatomical referent -- rule 15 already
    gives it a destination (Useless) regardless of anatomical legibility.
    Before finalizing any batch, mechanically check this invariant across
    all rows (semantic_status == "OPAQUE") == (taxonomy_status ==
    "NOT_APPLICABLE") and treat any violation as a miscoding to fix, not
    as evidence of a new exception category.
