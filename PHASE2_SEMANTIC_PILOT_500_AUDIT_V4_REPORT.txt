CORRECTED DEFINITIONS APPLIED (superseding V3's over-broad PARTIAL usage):
  CLEAR -- the complete literal has ONE stable core semantic reading at
    the level the literal actually specifies. CLEAR does NOT require
    knowing a subtype, an owner, a side, which specific finger, the exact
    mechanism of an expression, or what object a named item is attached
    to -- those are OMISSIONS, not semantic ambiguity.
  PARTIAL -- the literal contains meaningful semantic content BUT either
    (a) an actual meaningful token/relation in the literal remains
    genuinely undecoded, or (b) two or more MATERIALLY DIFFERENT core
    semantic readings remain plausible (e.g. "lock" = hair lock vs.
    mechanical lock; "bow" = decorative bow vs. archery bow vs. ship's
    bow; "plate" = armor vs. dinnerware vs. structural plate).
  OPAQUE -- no reliable core semantic referent/function can be
    established at all.
  The invariant OPAQUE <=> NOT_APPLICABLE is unchanged. CLEAR and PARTIAL
  may each pair with either taxonomy CLEAR or NEEDS_CONTEXT, depending on
  whether the known semantic information is sufficient to choose a
  destination -- this is a taxonomy question, never a reason by itself to
  downgrade semantic_status.
